Privacy Policy – Mirani App

Effective Date: November 19, 2026
Last Updated: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Mirani App. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Mirani App handles information when you use our mobile application.

Mirani App provides Islamic Prayer Times, a Qibla Compass, and eCodes. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about the information used by the application.

2. Information We Collect

Mirani App does not require users to create an account or provide information such as their name, email address, or phone number. Certain device information and permissions may be used to provide app functionality.

2.1 Location Information

Mirani App may request access to your device's location while you are using the application. Location may be used to determine your approximate geographical location and provide prayer times based on your location.

Location access is not intended to operate in the background. You can disable location permission at any time through your device settings. Some location-based features may not work correctly without this permission.

2.2 Device Sensors – Qibla Compass

The Qibla Compass feature uses your device's compass and orientation sensors to determine the direction of the Qibla. Sensor readings are processed on your device to provide compass functionality. Mirani App does not collect or store compass sensor readings for tracking purposes.

2.3 eCodes

Mirani App provides an eCodes feature. Information entered or accessed through this feature is used to provide its intended functionality. We do not intentionally collect personal information through this feature.

3. Third-Party Services

Mirani App uses a free third-party API to retrieve Islamic prayer time information. When requesting prayer times, information such as your selected city or location coordinates may be transmitted to the API provider, depending on how the service is configured.

The third-party provider may process information under its own privacy policy. We encourage users to review that provider's privacy policy. Mirani App does not control how third-party providers independently process information.

4. Advertising

Mirani App does not display advertisements. The application does not use Google AdMob or other third-party advertising networks.

5. Analytics and Tracking

Mirani App does not use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or other third-party analytics and tracking services. We do not intentionally track users' activities across applications or websites.

6. Data Storage and Security

Mirani App does not operate its own user account system or collect personal information for user profiling. Certain app settings or preferences may be stored locally on your device to support application functionality.

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information. Information may be transmitted to the third-party Prayer Times API only when necessary to provide the requested functionality. We do not intentionally share personal information with advertisers or analytics providers.

8. Children's Privacy

Mirani App is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our application, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

9. User Permissions and Controls

You can manage application permissions through your device settings, disable location access, restrict application permissions, or uninstall the application at any time. Disabling certain permissions may affect the availability or accuracy of some app features.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our application may use third-party services or APIs to provide certain functionality. These services operate independently and have their own privacy policies and terms of service. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party service providers.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in application features, services, or applicable legal requirements. Updates will be published on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date. Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
